MY STORY

Meet the nurse behind Pretty Devil

I’m Emma, an NMC-registered nurse, qualified since 2020, with a clinical background in Accident & Emergency, private healthcare and dermatology.

My nursing career has taught me the importance of precision, safety and, above all, treating every person as an individual.

Over the years, my passion naturally evolved towards aesthetics — an area where I could combine my clinical expertise with my love of beauty, detail and helping people feel confident in themselves. Pretty Devil Beauty is the result of that journey.

Why lash lift & tint?

There is something effortlessly beautiful about a perfectly lifted, defined lash. A lash lift and tint enhances your natural lashes, opens the appearance of the eyes and creates a polished look without the need for extensions or daily mascara.

I love it because the result is simple, elegant and low maintenance — a small treatment that can make you feel instantly more put together. For me, it's the perfect example of how lashes should be: subtle enhancement, beautiful results.

Why I chose aesthetics

I always believe that beauty is personal. Aesthetics, for me, is not about changing the way you look or following every passing trend. It is about enhancing the features that make you you.

I am drawn to treatments that are considered, refined and beautifully subtle — the kind of results that make you look refreshed and confident without taking away your individuality. My clinical background allows me to approach aesthetics with a deeper understanding of anatomy, skin health, safety and individual suitability, while my passion for beauty brings the creative eye behind each treatment.

Beyond the traditional nursing role

Nursing will always be part of my identity. But I reached a point in my career where I wanted to create something that felt truly personal — something that brought together everything I had learned professionally with something I genuinely loved.

Moving beyond a traditional nursing role has allowed me to slow the experience down. To have conversations rather than simply consultations. To understand what makes each individual feel confident. And to create a space where clients feel heard, comfortable and never pressured. I am not leaving my nursing values behind — I am bringing them with me.

My philosophy

I believe aesthetics should complement who you already are. My philosophy is centred on natural-looking results, individualised treatment and uncompromising attention to safety.

I will never believe that more is necessarily better. Sometimes the smallest, most carefully considered enhancement can make the biggest difference to how you feel. Every client is different. Every face is different. Every journey is different. That's why your treatment should be designed around you — never the other way around.

Welcome to Pretty Devil Beauty. Where clinical expertise meets modern beauty. Where subtle enhancement becomes confidence. And where you remain unapologetically you.

Why Choose a Lash Lift & Tint?

A lash lift and tint is one of the most effective ways to enhance your natural lashes without the need for extensions or daily mascara.

By lifting your own lashes from the root and adding a rich tint, your eyes appear brighter, more open, and beautifully defined.

The treatment creates the illusion of longer, fuller lashes while maintaining a soft, natural appearance. Results typically last 6–8 weeks, making it the perfect low-maintenance beauty treatment for busy lifestyles, holidays, or anyone wanting to wake up with effortlessly beautiful lashes every day.

Clients choose lash lift and tint treatments to:

  • Enhance the appearance of their natural lashes.
  • Create the look of longer, fuller lashes without extensions.
  • Open and brighten the eyes with a beautifully lifted effect.
  • Reduce the need for mascara and eyelash curlers.
  • Enjoy long-lasting, low-maintenance results.
  • Feel confident with naturally beautiful lashes from morning until night.

Anti-Wrinkle Treatments

Anti-wrinkle treatments work by relaxing targeted muscles responsible for creating expression lines, helping to soften existing wrinkles and prevent deeper lines from forming.

Frown Lines

Reduce the vertical lines between the eyebrows (often called "11 lines") for a smoother, more relaxed appearance.

Smokers' Lines

Reduce the fine vertical lines around the lips for a smoother, rejuvenated appearance.

Forehead Lines

Soften horizontal forehead lines while maintaining natural facial movement.

Bunny Lines

Soften the fine wrinkles that appear across the bridge of the nose when smiling or scrunching the face.

Crow's Feet

Minimise the fine lines around the outer corners of the eyes, creating a fresher, more youthful look.

Gummy Smile

Reduce excessive gum display when smiling by relaxing the muscles that elevate the upper lip.

Brow Lift

A subtle treatment that gently lifts the eyebrows, opening the eye area for a brighter, more refreshed appearance.

Downturned Smile

Lift the corners of the mouth to create a softer, more positive facial expression.

Jawline Slimming (Masseter)

Relax the masseter muscles to create a softer, slimmer jawline while also helping to reduce jaw clenching and teeth grinding.

Dimpled ('Pebbled') Chin

Smooth the chin by relaxing the muscle responsible for an uneven or dimpled appearance.

Nefertiti Neck Lift & Platysmal Bands

Improve the appearance of the jawline and neck by relaxing the platysmal muscles, creating a smoother and more defined profile.

Nose Tip Lift

A non-surgical treatment that gently lifts the tip of the nose for improved facial balance.

Excessive Sweating Treatment

Axillary Hyperhidrosis (Excessive Sweating)

Excessive underarm sweating can significantly impact confidence and daily life. Anti-wrinkle injections can temporarily reduce sweat production by blocking the signals sent to the sweat glands, helping you stay drier and more comfortable for several months.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers use hyaluronic acid to restore lost volume, enhance facial contours and soften deeper lines while maintaining natural-looking results.

Lip Fillers

Enhance lip shape, volume and definition while maintaining a beautifully balanced, natural appearance.

Cheek Volume Restoration

Restore lost volume to the cheeks, improving facial contours and creating a youthful, lifted appearance.

Nasolabial Folds

Soften the lines running from the nose to the corners of the mouth for a fresher, rejuvenated look.

Chin Augmentation

Enhance chin projection and definition to improve facial harmony and profile balance.

Marionette Lines

Reduce the lines extending from the corners of the mouth towards the chin to restore a smoother appearance.

Jawline Filler

Define and contour the jawline for improved facial structure and a sharper, more sculpted appearance.

Why Choose Aesthetic Treatments?

Modern aesthetic medicine is about far more than simply treating lines and wrinkles. It is about restoring confidence, preserving natural beauty, and helping you look as refreshed and vibrant as you feel.

As we age, the skin naturally loses collagen, elastin, and volume, resulting in softer facial contours, reduced skin quality, and the formation of fine lines. Carefully performed non-surgical aesthetic treatments can help restore balance, improve facial harmony, and create a naturally rejuvenated appearance without altering your unique identity.

Patients often choose aesthetic treatments to:

  • Soften the appearance of fine lines and dynamic wrinkles.
  • Restore youthful facial volume and definition.
  • Enhance natural facial features with subtle refinement.
  • Improve facial symmetry and overall balance.
  • Support graceful ageing with preventative treatments.
  • Feel more confident in both their personal and professional lives.

The most successful aesthetic treatments are those that leave others noticing how well you look, rather than wondering what you have had done.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

1. Who we are

Pretty Devil Beauty is a beauty and aesthetics business based in the UK. For the purposes of UK data protection legislation, Pretty Devil Beauty is the data controller responsible for your personal information. If you have any questions about how your information is handled, please contact us using the contact details provided on our website.

2. Information we collect

We may collect personal information that you provide to us when you contact us through our website, email, telephone or social media; make or enquire about an appointment; complete a consultation or consent form; receive a treatment from us; purchase products or services; subscribe to marketing communications; provide feedback or a testimonial; or give consent for photographs or videos to be taken.

Depending on the circumstances, the information we may collect includes:

Personal details — name, date of birth, address, telephone number, email address.

Appointment and treatment information — appointment history, treatment details, consultation information, treatment notes, consent records, aftercare information, and relevant preferences relating to your treatment.

Health and medical information — where necessary for the safe provision of treatments, we may collect information relating to your health, allergies, medications, medical conditions and other relevant contraindications. This information is treated as special category data under UK data protection law and will only be collected and used where necessary and with an appropriate lawful basis.

Photographs and media — where you have provided appropriate consent, we may take and use photographs or videos of treatments and results for purposes such as your treatment record, education, website content, social media and marketing. You can withdraw consent for marketing use of photographs or other media at any time.

3. How we use your information

We may use your personal information to manage and confirm appointments; provide treatments safely and appropriately; maintain accurate client and treatment records; communicate with you regarding your appointments; respond to enquiries; provide aftercare advice; process payments; manage cancellations and appointment changes; improve our services; respond to feedback or complaints; meet our legal, regulatory and professional obligations; protect the health and safety of our clients; send marketing communications where you have consented to receive them; and use photographs, videos or testimonials for marketing where you have given consent. We will only use your personal information where we have a lawful basis for doing so.

4. Lawful basis for processing

Under UK data protection law, we may process your personal information where you have given us your consent; processing is necessary to provide a service you have requested; processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation; processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided these interests do not override your rights; or processing is necessary to protect someone's vital interests.

Where we process health information or other special category data, we will ensure that an appropriate additional condition for processing is met. Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time.

If you have opted to receive marketing communications from Pretty Devil Beauty, we may contact you about new treatments, offers and promotions, availability, business updates, and events or special offers.

You can unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by contacting us or using the unsubscribe option where provided. We will not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.
